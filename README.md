## Overview

This project builds a two-part analytics suite — a **Sales Report** and a **Finance Report** — designed to surface actionable insights from raw business data. Each report targets a specific decision-making layer: operational sales performance and strategic financial planning.

---

## 📈 Sales Report

### What it does
- Tracks individual [customer performance](Sales_Division_Performance_Report.pdf) across fiscal periods
- Benchmarks actual sales against [predefined market targets](Sales_Top5_Country_Performance.pdf)
- Analyzes [new product launches](Sales_New_Product_Launch_2021.pdf) and their revenue impact
- Ranks [top 10 products](Sales_Top10_Product_Performance.pdf) by sales contribution
- Compares [best and worst performing segments](Sales_Top5_Bottom5_Comparison.pdf)

### Why it matters
- Surfaces revenue trends and KPIs that guide sales strategy
- Informs discount structures and customer negotiation frameworks
- Highlights high-potential markets for geographic expansion

---

## 💰 Finance Report

### What it does
- Generates P&L statements broken down by [fiscal year](Finance_PL_Statement_Fiscal_Year.pdf), [month](Finance_PL_Statement_Monthly.pdf), and [market](Finance_PL_Statement_By_Market.pdf)
- Tracks [gross margin percentage](Finance_Gross_Margin_Analysis.pdf) across business units

### Why it matters
- Provides a structured baseline for budget forecasting and peer benchmarking
- Aligns financial reporting with broader strategic objectives
- Builds stakeholder confidence through transparent, period-over-period analysis
